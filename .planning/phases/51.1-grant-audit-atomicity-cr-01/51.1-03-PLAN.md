<objective>
Subject the landed CR-01 fix to a fresh, non-self, orchestrator-owned adversarial code-trace, fold any findings, and record the verdict — satisfying ROADMAP Phase 51.1 success criterion 5.

Purpose: This repository's fresh-context adversarial review guardrail has caught real blocking and major defects through v1.9, and CR-01 itself was found by exactly this mechanism operating on Phase 51. The specific hazards for a change in this area are a verifier quietly loosened to make a test pass, a causal chain conflated with the provenance graph, and a regression that looks like an oracle but cannot fail. None of the three is reliably visible to the author.
Output: A trace brief, a trace record with a recorded verdict, and no change to any requirement, roadmap, state or ledger status.

Criterion 5 is deliberately NOT an executor task. A gsd-executor reading its own phase's diff is not clearance, and the standing precedent from Plan 51-08 makes reviewer independence a blocking gate rather than a documentation step. Per the user's standing direction, the reviewer is an external AI reviewer (Codex or Grok) so the independence is genuine rather than nominal.
