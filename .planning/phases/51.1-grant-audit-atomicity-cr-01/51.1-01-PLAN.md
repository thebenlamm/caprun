<objective>
Close CR-01: make the `session_grants` capability row and its `github_grant_authorized` audit event commit or roll back together, and prove it with a committed fault-injection regression that is demonstrated RED before the fix and GREEN after it.

Purpose: `record_github_grant` currently inserts the capability row in autocommit and then appends its authorization event as a second, independent transaction. A recoverable append failure leaves a durable, active `github.pr` grant with no authorization event in the tamper-evident chain, and the retry's `INSERT OR IGNORE` reports zero affected rows, silently skips the event and returns success. `has_github_grant` reads only the row, so `github.pr` dispatch can then proceed on a capability the audit chain never authorized.
Output: One new committed regression test binary carrying the oracle, and a scoped `crates/brokerd/src/` change that makes the two writes atomic — with the verifier and the provenance graph mechanically shown not to have moved.

Tasks 1 and 2 together are this phase's tracer slice: Task 1 lands the end-to-end oracle that traverses every layer this phase touches (`open_audit_db` to `record_github_grant` to `append_event` to the `session_grants` and `events` tables to `has_github_grant` and `verify_chain`) and proves it RED; Task 2 makes it GREEN. Task 3 discharges the two recorded comment-drift defects.
</objective>
