`email.send` sink, so CLAUDE.md's Phase-16 Mailpit rule is satisfied without a sidecar.

- **After every task commit:** `cargo test -p brokerd --lib audit:: -- --test-threads=1` (~1 s)

- **Before the fresh non-self adversarial trace (success criterion 5):** full local suite green
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** full local suite green
